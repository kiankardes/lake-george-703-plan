Check-in rules

  • Readiness under 25 or sleep under 5.5 h: easy day.
  • Readiness under 50, sleep under 7 h, HRV under 30, resting HR 8+ over normal, or soreness 4+: trim the hard set by a third and drop the PM.
  • Missed a week? Repeat it. Never stack missed sessions.

Two-a-days

  • One hard session per day, max. The other is easy or strength.
  • 6+ hours between sessions; eat carbs and protein within 30 min of the AM.
  • Sleep under 7 h: cut the PM first.

Fueling

  • Rides over 2 h: 60–90 g carbs per hour, 500–750 ml fluid with sodium.
  • Sweat test: weigh before and after a 1 h ride. Each kg lost ≈ 1 L of sweat.
  • From week 21, use only your race-day products.

Race day

Swim · 40 min budget. Likely wetsuit-legal in early June. Start steady, sight every 6–8 strokes, find feet.

Bike · 2:40 budget (~21 mph). Cap climbs near FTP. Eat on a timer.

Run · 1:34 budget (7:10/mi). First 3 mi at ≤155 bpm, settle at 150–160, race the last 5K.

Goal 5:00: 40 min swim, 6 min of transitions, 2:40 bike, 1:34 run. The Road to 5:00 bars at the top track it live. The 2027 bike course isn't published yet; check the official course and match its climbing in your long rides.

Rehearse all of it in the May 22 simulation. Nothing new on race day.

What-ifsFlat: change it calmly (practice at home). Cramp: slow down, drink, salt. Upset gut: water only for 15 min, then carbs slowly. Swim panic: roll onto your back, breathe, restart.

Sources: Thomas, Erdman & Burke, ACSM joint position statement on nutrition and athletic performance (2016); Guest et al., ISSN position stand on caffeine and exercise performance (2021); Jeukendrup, carbohydrate intake during exercise (Sports Med, 2014).

Climbs near Middlebury

For the hilly long rides in Build and Peak (wk 23, 25, 29). The 2027 bike course isn't published yet, so use these to build climbing strength and practice holding steady power uphill. Gap roads can close or get snowy in winter; check conditions first.

  • Middlebury Gap (VT-125 east through Ripton): long, steady climb. Good for race-power blocks.
  • Brandon Gap (VT-73): steady grade, quieter road.
  • Appalachian Gap (VT-17): harder; save for late Build.
  • Lincoln Gap: very steep, closed in winter. Strength work, not race-pace practice.

The Today tab suggests routes from your own past rides that match each long ride's length (hilly sessions favor your hilliest ones). For new routes, build them in Ride with GPS or the Strava route builder and send them to your Garmin.
