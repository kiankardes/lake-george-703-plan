# Kian — IRONMAN 70.3 Lake George training guide

**Race:** June 13, 2027. **Primary:** finish under 5 hours; **stretch:** roughly 4:45–4:50 if demonstrated in training. Swim aspiration **1:45/100 m** and run aspiration **7:00/mile** are *race goals*, not training thresholds or current proven race splits.

## How to use the site
- **Sunday, 5 minutes:** inspect This Week and move sessions around work or recovery constraints as needed.
- **Training day:** read Today's warm-up, intervals, intensity, cooldown, and why. Bike on Zwift/KICKR; run on treadmill or outdoors; swim off the displayed pool set. Direct Garmin workout delivery is optional, not required.
- **After:** Garmin syncs through intervals.icu. Mark sessions/log RPE only if valuable. No daily CTL or dashboard obsession.
- **Every ~4 weeks when requested:** review adherence, how the back handled load, and the latest benchmark; change the next block. The current website cannot autonomously obtain new coaching decisions from ChatGPT without a new request.
- **Training duration target:** around **12 hours swim+bike+run+squash** on developed weeks; *three lifts on top*. Deload weeks intentionally lower. No need to hit 12 hours exactly.

## Fixed weekly pattern after return to full training
| Day | AM | PM or flexible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Rest/recovery | Long conversational Z2 bike (eventually 2.5–3+ hours when practical) |
| Tue | Pull + Legs lift | Swim threshold/pace (or controlled CSS repetitions) |
| Wed | Easy-to-steady run | Squash (counts as a hard session) |
| Thu | Push + Legs lift | Swim aerobic endurance |
| Fri | Swim technique | Zwift structured bike intervals |
| Sat | Upper lift | Easy bike followed by a short relaxed brick run |
| Sun | Long easy run | Rest |

**Three weekly runs** includes the short Saturday brick. A stronger Wednesday tempo run sometimes *replaces squash* in the later phase; do **not** stack both hard. Some weeks include fewer than 12 hours by design.

## Return-to-training gate — current low-back injury
The Foundation calendar is **not clearance**. If sitting, bending, riding, running, or swimming still provokes back pain—or if symptoms increase later that day or next morning—repeat easier activity, substitute comfortable walking, and consult the treating clinician/physical therapist. Avoid heavy leg loading, vigorous trunk rotation, hard intervals, and competitive squash until function and movement have recovered. New radiating leg symptoms, weakness, or neurological symptoms warrant clinical reassessment; bowel/bladder changes or saddle numbness require urgent care.

The December-to-January rebuild stays conservative; weeks with threshold efforts are conditional on full, assessed return. The step up into the next phase is not mandatory. Replace any irritating activity with an easier approved alternative; do not chase a dashboard percentage.

## Provisional training zones — update only from tests, not goal times
- **Bike:** FTP baseline ~290 W (user-reported, update using a reliable test when back allows). Zone 2 on this site: 56–75% FTP (about 162–218 W). Sweet spot 88–94% (~255–273 W). Threshold 95–100% (~276–290 W). Race power provisional 76–81% (~220–235 W); this is a training range to *validate* during sustained rides and fueling tests, not a promised course split.
- **Run:** Conversational easy typically around 8:15–9:00/mi with HR ~130–140 when feeling normal. Use RPE/HR if pace varies, and never force this during rehab. 7:00/mi is an eventual *race ambition*. Determine a realistic tempo and race-pace progression after an uninterrupted block of pain-free running and controlled benchmarks.
- **Swim:** 1:45/100 m open-water ambition. Measured CSS should come from a 400 m/200 m pool test when appropriate. Reps at CSS, CSS +5 seconds, easy drill work and longer aerobic repeats have distinct roles. Pool length matters: 1:45/100 m ≈ 1:36/100 yards.

## Progressive overload
Each month includes 2–3 developing weeks and 1 easier week. Raise one major variable at a time—e.g. 3×8 → 3×10 → 3×12 minutes sweet spot, not simultaneous major hikes in watts and overall time. The three swims target technique/breathing, sustainable 200–400 m repeats, and controlled pace repeats. Wednesday squash already creates high intensity; the bike generally gets one quality interval day. Longer runs progress mostly **by easy time on feet**, not by faster pace.

## Loading and recovery
- Squash is counted in the endurance-hour budget and toward high-intensity stress.
- The existing Pull+Legs, Push+Legs, and Upper lifts remain, with reduced sets during recovery and late-season race preparation. More lifting is not always better when long-run and bike volume rise.
- Hard cycling workouts use dynamic site FTP percentages. Swim paces use site CSS. Both change when the user updates a verified test.
- Start race nutrition practice on longer rides, including liquids and carbohydrate tolerance; don't trial new products on race day.
- Avoid forcing 12h in a busy work or injury week. Consistency over months is the objective.
